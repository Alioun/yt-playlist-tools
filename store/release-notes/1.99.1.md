The first beta of Perchling for YouTube™ 2.0, the rebuild of YouTube Playlist Tools.
- On Firefox, install it over 1.4.5 from https://alioun.github.io/yt-playlist-tools/beta/. Firefox treats that as an update. Your 1.x playlists, shortcut, watch percentage and toast setting carry over, and nothing runs until you've reviewed them on the page that opens.
- Later betas arrive by themselves. Once 2.0.0 is out on Firefox Add-ons, the last beta update moves you to it.
- Every feature starts off. Switch on the ones you want to try.
- Problems and ideas go to https://github.com/Alioun/yt-playlist-tools/issues/new/choose (needs a GitHub account).
- Needs Firefox 140 or later, or Chrome 121 or later.
