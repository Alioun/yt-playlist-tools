Perch

The 2.0 icon: a pigeon sits on top of the pigeonholes, not in one. Hand-drawn SVG; the 16 and 32 px files are their own pixel-grid drawings. Every image on this page is the exported PNG that ships. The name is Perchling; the wordmark and lockups are in assets/brand/.

Every size

16 and 32 are drawn on their own grids; 19 and 24 come from the 16 and 32 drawings; 48 and up use the full artwork. Chrome's 19 and 38 are legacy and needn't ship.

  • One icon for light and dark. Chrome can't switch icons by theme, so the large shapes sit near 3:1 on every toolbar. The grey bird, the teal ledge and the light-violet cabinet carry the silhouette; the dark holes and the tail are detail.
  • No Firefox dark variant. context-fill is reserved for Mozilla's own extensions, and a theme_icons pair would gain little over these ratios while making Firefox differ from Chrome.
