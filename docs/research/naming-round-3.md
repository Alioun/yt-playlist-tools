# Naming, round 3: pigeonhole

Research for [ticket 22](../../.scratch/rebuild/issues/22-name.md), round 3. Every check ran on 2026-10-20 with [round 2's](naming-round-2.md) method and collision rules, except where [Limits](#limits) says otherwise. Store searches are fuzzy, and a register search isn't legal clearance.

## Answer

- **The ask:** you liked the pigeonhole grid and want a name built on "pigeonhole", not tied to "Playlist", with a "not to be pigeonholed" wink.
- **Screened:** 51 names.
  - 5 bare forms;
  - 28 "Pigeonhole + word" compounds;
  - 10 puns;
  - 8 close cousins, as a backup.
- **Bare "Pigeonhole" collides,** and so do "Pigeonholes", "Pigeon Hole", "The Pigeonhole" and "Pigeonholed". Several things already use the name:
  - a Chrome listing "PigeonHole" and a Google Play app "Pigeonhole";
  - the App Store book app "The Pigeonhole";
  - Dovecot's Pigeonhole (100 GitHub stars);
  - a Spotify playlist-filing app called pigeonhole, with the same pun.
- **Five derived words pass every check,** even under round 2's strictest rule: Unpigeonholed, Not Pigeonholed, Pigeonholer, Unpigeonhole and Pigeonholing.
- **Every "Pigeonhole + word" compound is clean on the stores, the app stores, the registers and the web.** One judgment call remains: three hobby repos use "pigeonhole" for music or video. Round 2's word rule would count one of them, a 2-star homelab template. [Details](#the-judgment-call-pigeonhole-in-music-and-video).
- **Pigeonhole Live and PigeonPod don't block the finalists.** [Verdicts](#pigeonhole-live-and-pigeonpod).
- **Recommendation:** **Unpigeonholed for YouTube™**. It carries the wink in one word, passes every check under the strict rules, and both `unpigeonholed.com` and `.app` are free. If you'd rather start with "Pigeonhole", take **Pigeonhole Post for YouTube™** and accept the judgment call. [Why](#recommendation).

**Verdict: collides.** Round 2's rule rejects a name when a listing, app or repo uses the same name in any field, and here several do. No live registration blocks it in software classes, so this is a crowding problem more than a legal one. Even so, a "Pigeonhole for YouTube™" listing would sit next to a "PigeonHole" listing on the same store.

## Pigeonhole Live and PigeonPod

**Pigeonhole Live** ([pigeonhole.at](https://pigeonhole.at), pigeonholelive.com):
- **What it is:** an audience-engagement platform (live Q&A, polls, quizzes) for meetings and events, with Microsoft Teams and PowerPoint add-ins.
- **Who runs it:** PigeonLab Pte Ltd, Singapore, since 2010.
- **Marks:** no PIGEONHOLE LIVE or PIGEONLAB mark on USPTO, EUIPO or WIPO Madrid, and no store listing or app turned up in our searches. Singapore's register and TMview couldn't be checked.
- **Verdict:**
  - **Not a collision for the finalists.** Event audience engagement isn't video, music or playlists, and no mark of theirs was found whose goods overlap ours.
  - **It adds weight against the bare name.** "Pigeonhole" is their brand word in software, and their Singapore filings are unknown.

**PigeonPod** ([github.com/aizhimou/pigeon-pod](https://github.com/aizhimou/pigeon-pod), 1,196 stars) turns YouTube channels and playlists into podcast feeds. It holds no mark in the US, the EU or Madrid.
- **Verdict: not a collision.** Its brand word is "Pigeon", the bird, plus "Pod". "Pigeonhole" is a different word, a compartment, and the bird is buried in it.
- **Judgment call:** both start with the same six letters, and both touch YouTube. The risk is low, and lower still with an icon that shows no bird. The grid does that.
- **Effect on the shortlist:** names with "Pigeon" as a word of its own stay out, as in round 2. "Pigeon Post" fails here for other reasons anyway.

## The judgment call: "pigeonhole" in music and video

Three hobby repos use "pigeonhole" as a name in our field:
- **`ashleyjwu/pigeonhole`:** a personal Spotify web app (0 stars, created 2026-07-31). It suggests the best-fit playlist for a song and files it in one tap, with the same pun.
- **`miadugas/Pigeonhole`:** "music organization for music lovers" (1 star, last push 2022).
- **`thotsl4yer69/pigeonhole`:** "Pigeonhole Streaming", a homelab streaming site with a template README (2 stars, active).

None is on a store, and none has a mark.

Round 2's word rule counts an in-field project with at least 1 star and a push in the last year. To the letter, that catches the 2-star template, and with it every "Pigeonhole + word" name. The same rule rejected Playlist Wren and Playlist Quokka over similar small repos.

My judgment: treat the three as notes, not collisions, for compounds. None is a product, and a compound like "Pigeonhole Post" is a different name. The Spotify app does show that the idea and the pun aren't unique. **The derived words (Unpigeonholed, Pigeonholer and the rest) don't depend on this call.**

## Finalists

All names are "… for YouTube™". Chars count the full store name; Chrome recommends a `short_name` of 12 or fewer. Every finalist suits the hand-drawn pigeonhole-grid icon, and none needs YouTube's red or play shapes. "Clean" means no listing, app, repo, mark or web product with the name. Each also has nothing in the field that uses its main word as a brand, except the judgment call above for the B names.

### Finding it on the stores without "Playlist"

- **The trade-off:** round 1 kept "Playlist" first because it's the word people search for. None of these names has it.
- **Where the search word lives instead:** round 2's market check found that the name earns little reach and the summary does the finding. Both drafted summaries in [spec 08 §9.1](../../.scratch/rebuild/spec/08-release.md) already carry "playlist":
  - **AMO summary (246 of 250):** "Save YouTube videos to Watch Later or any playlist in one click or with a shortcut, auto-add the videos you actually watch, and get one-click Play next and Add to queue on YouTube Music. Every feature is opt-in. (Formerly YouTube Playlist Tools.)"
  - **Chrome `description` (132 of 132):** "Save to Watch Later or any playlist in one click or a shortcut, auto-add what you actually watch, and Add to queue on YouTube Music."
- **Optional: put "playlist" in the store name with a subtitle.** This follows the "Name: descriptor for YouTube" pattern that listings like "Playlist Wrangler: Manager for YouTube" use. Inside the popup and settings, the name stays short.
  - "Unpigeonholed: Playlist Saver for YouTube™" (42 chars, under AMO's 50)
  - "Pigeonhole Post: Playlist Saver for YouTube™" (44)
  - "Pigeonholer: Playlist Saver for YouTube™" (40)
- **The subtitle's cost:** it narrows the name back towards playlists, the very thing you wanted to avoid. Use one descriptor at most; Chrome's spam rules punish keyword lists.
- **The GatherTube port:** neither summary mentions gathering tabs yet ([ticket 31](../../.scratch/rebuild/issues/31-gathertube.md)). That belongs in the description, not the name.

## Recommendation

1. **Unpigeonholed for YouTube™.**
   - It's the name you described: the pigeonhole idea, not tied to playlists, with the wink built in.
   - It's the only strong candidate that passes even under round 2's strictest reading.
   - Both domains are free, and the grid icon gets a natural twist: one card popping out of its slot.
   - **Costs:** 13 letters, one over Chrome's `short_name` advice, and it doesn't say what it does. The summary carries that, or the optional subtitle does.
2. **Pigeonhole Post for YouTube™,** if you'd rather lead with "Pigeonhole".
   - It says the job (post each thing into its slot) and both domains are free.
   - It depends on [the judgment call](#the-judgment-call-pigeonhole-in-music-and-video).
3. **Pigeonholer for YouTube™,** if you want it short (11 letters).
   - Clean under the strict rules.
   - It names the job but not the wink.

**Not recommended:** bare "Pigeonhole" (it collides), and "Pigeonholed", whose pun is already used by a playlist-filing app.

## Limits

- **TMview was still blocked** at 04:37, about 95 minutes after its firewall first answered with a challenge page. EUIPO and WIPO Madrid replaced its EU and international coverage. National registers outside the US and EU weren't checked: China, Japan, Korea, the UK, Canada, Australia and Singapore, Pigeonhole Live's home.
- **GitHub, the stores and the web found three hobby "pigeonhole" music or video projects.** How to count them is [a judgment call](#the-judgment-call-pigeonhole-in-music-and-video).
- **Store searches are fuzzy, and the web search is US-only.** None of this is legal advice; a professional search for the chosen name is the safe step before launch.
