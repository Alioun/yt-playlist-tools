# Naming, round 4: pigeon

Research for [ticket 22](../../.scratch/rebuild/issues/22-name.md), round 4. Every check ran on 2026-10-20 with [round 2's](naming-round-2.md) method and rules, with one change: the PigeonPod overlap is weighed, not treated as an automatic kill. Store searches are fuzzy, and a register search isn't legal clearance.

> **Outcome (2026-10-20):** after round 5, the user chose **Perchling for YouTube™** ([ticket 22](../../.scratch/rebuild/issues/22-name.md), [ADR-0013](../adr/0013-named-perchling-for-youtube.md)). This note is kept as the record.

## Answer

- **The ask:** you rejected round 3 ("those are all awful, pigeon should be the direction"). This round shortlists names led by the bird. It favours short, warm names that sound like a product.
- **Screened:** 64 pigeon-led names: the plain "Pigeon", 26 compounds, 18 pigeon words and pet forms, 13 playful names and 5 "Playlist + pigeon word" names.
- **The overlaps weigh less than an automatic kill, but more than nothing:**
  - **PigeonPod** is the only real "Pigeon" product in YouTube. It's a commercial service, so it matters most.
  - **`playlist-pigeon`** is a repo dormant since 2022.
  - **Pigeon Music** is an Android radio app.
  - **PigeonCast,** a casting app with 100K+ downloads, turned up too. It holds a registered US mark in class 9, so it's rated as well.
  - [Weights](#how-much-the-overlaps-weigh).
- **Playlist Pigeon for YouTube™, re-checked today:** still clean on every store, both app stores, GitHub, USPTO, WIPO Madrid and the web. **Honest rating: low to medium, workable.** [Details](#playlist-pigeon-re-checked).
- **11 finalists** pass everything else under round 2's strict rules.
- **Top three:** Plump Pigeon, Pigeon Courier, Playlist Pigeon. [Why](#top-three).
- **Bare "Pigeon for YouTube™" collides,** independently of PigeonPod. [Why](#the-plain-name-pigeon).
- **Checks that couldn't run:** EUIPO joined TMview behind the same firewall this round, so EU marks weren't checked today. [Limits](#limits).

**What each weighs:**
- **PigeonPod:** the real one. It's a paid product for the same people, YouTube power users, and it deals in YouTube playlists. Its pricing is even counted in "YouTube playlist" episodes. It isn't an extension and holds no mark, so the risk is confusion, not law.
  - Confusion is likeliest when "Pigeon" leads our name, or when the name pairs "Pigeon" with "playlist".
  - It would rise if PigeonPod shipped a browser extension.
- **`playlist-pigeon`:** four years dormant, never launched. It matters only to the exact name "Playlist Pigeon", and only a little.
- **Pigeon Music:** a different product (a radio app) on a different platform. It shares "Pigeon" and "music", nothing more.
- **PigeonCast:** bigger than the others, and its name is a registered US mark for media-casting software. It casts screens to TVs, which isn't our job. It weighs most against a name built like it: "Pigeon" plus a media word.

**Rating scale** used below:
- **None:** no shared word or sound.
- **Low:** the word is shared, but the field, product type or name structure differs, so confusion is unlikely.
- **Medium:** "Pigeon" leads the name, or is fused into it the way "PigeonPod" is, in the YouTube space. Confusion is possible among YouTube power users.
- **High:** the same or nearly the same name in the same space.

**Honest rating: low to medium, and workable.**
- **Legal risk is low:** no mark for the name, no PIGEON mark with overlapping goods, and PigeonPod holds no registration.
- **Confusion risk is medium-low,** and it comes almost entirely from PigeonPod: two "Pigeon" tools that both touch YouTube playlists.
- **Keeping it low:**
  - keep the icon away from PigeonPod's slate pigeon with a striped wing (Cabinet bird already does);
  - always write "for YouTube™";
  - leave podcasts out of the copy.
- **What hasn't changed:** it's still the most descriptive name here. It still leads with playlists, which you once said you didn't want to be limited by.

## Top three

1. **Plump Pigeon for YouTube™.**
   - Short, warm, and it sounds like a product, not a pun.
   - The bird leads the feeling but not the brand: "Plump" comes first, so the PigeonPod overlap is the lowest of any name with "Pigeon" in it.
   - Both domains are free, and it names no single job.

   - Your original. It says the job, keeps the search word, and its domains are free.
   - Its cost: it leads with playlists, and its PigeonPod overlap is medium.

**Close behind:** Clever Pigeon and Good Pigeon (warm, low overlap) and Pigeon Desk (tidy, a strong Cabinet-bird fit).

## The plain name "Pigeon"

**"Pigeon for YouTube™" collides,** whatever PigeonPod weighs:
- **Chrome Web Store:** already has a listing called "Pigeon", a discussions-and-insights tool with 97 users, and another "Pigeon" extension for e-mailing elected officials.
- **USPTO:** live PIGEON marks cover downloadable software (Pigeon Medical's patient messaging, Pigeon Loans' spreadsheet app). For a one-word name, any identical live software mark counts.
- **Mo Willems' THE PIGEON!** is a live mark that includes children's videos.
- **Domains:** `pigeon.com` and `.app` are taken.

Its overlaps would be PigeonPod **high**, `playlist-pigeon` low and Pigeon Music **medium**.

## Limits

- **EUIPO and TMview didn't run.** EUIPO, which stood in for TMview in round 3, now answers with the same firewall challenge. So EU and national marks outside the US weren't checked today; WIPO Madrid covers only international registrations. UKIPO, DPMA, IP Australia, Singapore and WIPO's Global Brand Database stay unchecked, as in round 3.
- **Overlap ratings are judgments.** They're based on the facts in [the table above](#how-much-the-overlaps-weigh). PigeonPod's weight would rise if it shipped a browser extension or filed a mark.
- **Store searches are fuzzy, and the web search is US-only.** None of this is legal advice. A professional search for the chosen name is the safe step before launch, covering the EU above all.
