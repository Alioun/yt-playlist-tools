# Naming, round 5: perch

Research for [ticket 22](../../.scratch/rebuild/issues/22-name.md), round 5. Every check ran on 2026-10-20 with [round 2's](naming-round-2.md) strict rules. Where "Pigeon" appears, it also uses [round 4's](naming-round-4.md) weighed overlaps for PigeonPod, `playlist-pigeon` and Pigeon Music. Store searches are fuzzy, and a register search isn't legal clearance.

> **Outcome (2026-10-20):** the user chose A2, **Perchling for YouTube™** ([ticket 22](../../.scratch/rebuild/issues/22-name.md), [ADR-0013](../adr/0013-named-perchling-for-youtube.md)). The EU check in [Limits](#limits) is still due, as a professional search before launch. This note is kept as the record.

## Answer

- **The ask:** after round 4 you said "what about something with perch". The icon is now **Perch** ([ticket 34](../../.scratch/rebuild/issues/34-icon.md)): a pigeon perched on top of a shelf of pigeonholes with filed cards. You like the pigeon, and you disliked clunky puns.
- **Screened:** 56 names.
  - 7 plain forms;
  - 28 "Perch + word" compounds;
  - 5 coinages;
  - 8 bird-led names;
  - 8 others in the same spirit (shelf, ledge, sill, roost).
- **Bare "Perch for YouTube™" collides.**
  - Chrome already has seven extensions called "Perch". Two of them save or search bookmarks.
  - Edge has "Perch – Picture in Picture for every video", a YouTube video tool updated last week.
  - The live US mark PERCH (GM Labs) covers an app for reading, organising and storing saved content: Perch Reader.
  - [Verdict](#the-bare-name-perch).
- **"Playlist Perch" and other "Perch + word" names don't escape it.** "Perch" stays the brand word, so the same listings and marks apply. Under round 2's rules they fail; they pass only if you accept [the judgment call](#the-judgment-call).
- **Names whose brand isn't the bare word "Perch" do escape.** That covers "Pigeon Perch", "Pigeon's Perch" and "Pigeon Shelf", and the derived words "Perchling", "Perchy" and "Percher".
- **11 finalists:** 6 clean under the strict rules, and 5 that depend on the judgment call.
- **Top three:** Pigeon Perch, Perchling, Plump Perch. [Why](#top-three).
- **Checks that couldn't run:** TMview and EUIPO (still blocked at 05:40). [Limits](#limits).

## The judgment call

**Does a compound escape the bare-word conflict?** It depends on whether "Perch" is still the brand word.

- **"Playlist Perch" and "Perch + word" don't escape.**
  - "Playlist", "Box", "Tray", "Shelf" and the like are descriptive, so "Perch" carries the brand.
  - Round 2's rule then applies: a store listing uses the main word as its brand for a video tool (the Edge PiP extension). Round 2 rejected Playlist Perch for exactly that.
  - The registered PERCH mark for organising saved content adds a real legal factor.
- **Adjective + Perch (Plump, Snug, Little, Top) mostly doesn't escape:** a soft adjective leaves "Perch" as the brand noun.
- **"Pigeon Perch", "Pigeon's Perch" and "Pigeon Shelf" escape.** Both words are distinctive, so the pair is the brand. Nobody uses it, and "Pigeon" brings round 4's weighed overlaps instead.
- **Derived words (Perchling, Perchy, Percher) escape the strict rule,** as round 3's derived words did. A fused "Perch-" word still reads close to the Perch brands, so their overlap is rated medium.

**Weighing the Edge listing instead of auto-killing:**
- It's small: no ratings, no visible installs, Edge only. We don't list on Edge.
- But it's active, it's squarely a YouTube video tool, and it carries the exact word.
- Together with Perch Reader's registered mark, that's more weight than round 4 gave PigeonPod.
- So "Perch + word" names are shown below as a separate group. Their cost is stated rather than hidden.

## Finalists

All names are "… for YouTube™". Chars count the full store name. Each finalist has two sets of overlap ratings:
- **Pigeon overlaps,** where the name contains "Pigeon": PigeonPod / `playlist-pigeon` / Pigeon Music, on round 4's scale.
- **Perch overlaps:** the Edge "Perch" PiP extension / Perch Reader and the PERCH marks / Chrome's seven "Perch" extensions.

On both scales:
- **None:** no shared word.
- **Low:** the shared word isn't the brand.
- **Medium:** the shared word is the brand, or fused into it, in or next to our field.
- **High:** the same name in the same space.

## Top three

1. **Pigeon Perch for YouTube™.**
   - The icon in two words: the pigeon on its perch of pigeonholes.
   - Warm, plain, sounds like a product, no pun.
   - Clean under the strict rules, because the pair is the brand.
   - **Cost:** "Pigeon" leads, so the PigeonPod overlap is medium, as for round 4's Pigeon Courier. `pigeonperch.app` is free.
2. **Perchling for YouTube™.**
   - One soft word for a small bird on its perch, short, and clean under the strict rules.
   - **Cost:** it reads close to the "Perch" brands (medium overlap). `perchling.app` is free.
3. **Plump Perch for YouTube™.**
   - Round 4's favourite word with the perch, and both domains are free.
   - **Cost:** it's in group B, because "Perch" stays the brand word: the Edge "Perch" video extension, Perch Reader's mark and Chrome's "Perch" crowd.

**Close behind:** Pigeon Shelf (exact icon match, both domains free, no Perch overlap) and Playlist Perch (descriptive, group B).
