# Setting up 1.4.5 from Firefox Add-ons

[← Legacy](README.md) · **2.0 won't need any of this:** it uses your signed-in YouTube session instead of Google credentials. It isn't out yet ([2.0 planning](../rebuild/README.md)).

1.4.5 edits your playlists through Google's YouTube Data API, so it needs a Google sign-in "client" that you create yourself. It takes about 15 minutes, once. Use the Google account that owns your playlists.

## A. Google Cloud

1. **Create a project.** Open [console.cloud.google.com/projectcreate](https://console.cloud.google.com/projectcreate), enter any name and click **Create**. Keep that project selected at the top of the page for the next steps.
2. **Enable the API.** Open [YouTube Data API v3](https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/library/youtube.googleapis.com) in the API Library and click **Enable**. It must be **YouTube Data API v3**, not another YouTube API.
3. **Set up the consent screen.** Open [Google Auth Platform → Branding](https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/branding) and click **Get started**:
   - App Information: any app name, and your address as **User support email**. Click **Next**.
   - Audience: **External**. Click **Next**.
   - Contact Information: your address. Click **Next**.
   - Finish: tick the policy box, click **Continue**, then **Create**.
4. **Add the YouTube scope.** In the left menu, click **Data Access**, then **Add or remove scopes**. Filter for `youtube.force-ssl`, tick `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl`, click **Update**, then **Save**.
5. **Choose who can sign in.** New apps start in "Testing". Pick one, on the [Audience](https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/audience) page:
   - **Stay in Testing:** under **Test users**, click **Add users**, enter your Google address and click **Save**. The catch: Google expires your sign-in after 7 days, so you'll have to [re-authorise](#re-authorising-after-7-days) every week.
   - **Publish:** click **Publish app** and confirm. There's no 7-day expiry. The catch: Google hasn't verified your app, so each time you authorise, it warns "Google hasn't verified this app", and it caps unverified apps at 100 users. That's fine for your own use, and you don't need to apply for verification. Do this before step 10.
6. **Create the client.** Open [Clients](https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/clients) and click **Create client**. Set **Application type** to **Web application** and enter any name. Under **Authorized redirect URIs**, click **Add URI** and paste exactly this, with no trailing slash:

   ```
   http://127.0.0.1/mozoauth2/3674d031f91c4d1f63992b40b32667c173e578cd
   ```

   Click **Create**. This address is the same for every 1.4.5 install, because Firefox builds it from the add-on's fixed ID. 1.4.5 also shows it at the top of its options page, under **Redirect URI:**. If the two ever differ, use the options page's.
7. **Copy the Client ID and Client secret** from the dialog that opens. Google shows the secret only this once, so keep it somewhere safe or download the JSON. If you lose it, open the client and add a new secret.

## B. 1.4.5's options page

8. Open `about:addons`, click **YouTube Playlist Tools**, then the **Options** tab (**Preferences** on macOS and Linux).
9. Paste into **Client ID:** and **Client Secret:**, with no spaces. They save as you type; there's no Save button.
10. Click **Authorize**. In the Google window, pick the account, or YouTube channel, that owns your playlists. If it warns "Google hasn't verified this app", click **Continue** (or **Advanced**, then **Go to** *your app name* **(unsafe)**), then allow access. The button changes to **Authorized** and greys out.

## C. 1.4.5's popup

11. **Find a playlist ID.** Open the playlist on YouTube. Its address looks like `https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx`, and the ID is everything after `list=`, up to any `&`. The playlist must belong to the account you authorised. Watch Later can't be used, because the Data API doesn't allow it.
12. **Fill in the popup.** Click the Extensions button (the puzzle piece) in the toolbar, then **YouTube Playlist Tools**. Everything saves as you type.
    - **Playlists to auto add current video to:** paste an ID into **Playlist ID**. **Add Playlist** adds another box.
    - **Add to playlist shortcut:** paste an ID into **Playlist ID**, click **Record Shortcut** and press your keys, for example Alt+Shift+A. It then shows "Shortcut: Alt+Shift+A". Don't use Ctrl: 1.4.5 never matches a shortcut that includes it.
    - **Required Watch Percentage:** move the slider. Until you change it once, 1.4.5 ignores the 50 it shows and adds every video as soon as it starts.
    - **Enable Toast Notifications:** tick it. Toasts are off by default, and without them 1.4.5 shows nothing at all.
13. **Test it.** Play a video past your percentage, then reload the playlist. 1.4.5 shows its "Added …" toast even when the add failed, so check the playlist itself.

Auto-add and the shortcut work on normal watch pages (`youtube.com/watch?v=…`), not on Shorts or YouTube Music.

## Troubleshooting

### Re-authorising after 7 days

If your app is in Testing, the sign-in expires a week after you authorise. Videos then stop reaching your playlists, with no toast, and the options page still says **Authorized**. The **Authorize** button stays disabled until you click **Clear Storage**, which erases every setting. So:

1. Copy the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** out of the options page. Note your playlist IDs and shortcut from the popup.
2. Open [myaccount.google.com/linkedapps](https://myaccount.google.com/linkedapps), choose your app, then **Remove access** and **Confirm**. Google sends the long-lived token only on a fresh grant, and 1.4.5 doesn't ask for one again.
3. Optional: publish the app (step 5) now, so this doesn't happen again.
4. On the options page, click **Clear Storage**, paste the Client ID and Client Secret back, and click **Authorize** (step 10).
5. In the popup, re-enter your playlist IDs, re-record the shortcut, set the percentage and tick toasts again.

### Other problems

- **"No access token found" toast:** you haven't authorised yet (step 10).
- **An "Added …" toast, but the playlist hasn't changed:** check that YouTube Data API v3 is enabled (step 2), and that the ID is right, belongs to the account you authorised, and isn't Watch Later. The API also allows only about 200 adds a day (10,000 quota units, at 50 per add), reset at midnight Pacific time.
- **"Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch" in the Google window:** the URI from step 6 doesn't exactly match the options page's **Redirect URI:**. Fix it, then wait: Google says changes take from 5 minutes to a few hours.
- **"Access blocked: … has not completed the Google verification process":** the account you picked isn't a test user. Add it (step 5), or publish the app.
- **You allowed access, but the button still says Authorize:** Google sent no long-lived token. Remove the app's access ([re-authorising](#re-authorising-after-7-days), step 2) and click **Authorize** again.
